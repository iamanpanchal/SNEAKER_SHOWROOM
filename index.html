Our Story

BORN FROM
THE
STREETS

YP Kicks started in a tiny rented garage in Ahmedabad in 2021. Two broke design students, one sewing machine, and a completely unreasonable amount of confidence. We had no investors, no influencer deals, no clue what we were doing — just a genuine obsession with how a good pair of shoes can change the whole energy of a fit.

"We didn't make shoes. We made statements you wear on your feet."

The name? YP stands for "Your Pair" — because we believe sneakers are deeply personal. The right pair isn't just footwear, it's an extension of who you are on a Tuesday at 2pm when nobody's watching.

Every YP drop is designed in-house, crafted in small batches, and released with the kind of intentionality that big brands literally cannot afford to have. We stay small on purpose. Because limited means something here.
